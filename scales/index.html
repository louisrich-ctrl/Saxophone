Saxophone scales in every key

Notes and fingerings for the main scales in all 12 keys. The keys are written pitch, so the fingerings are the same on alto, soprano and tenor.

Major scales

The major scale is the bright, happy-sounding scale that most beginner tunes use. It is the first scale most teachers set, and the best place to start.

CD♭DE♭EFF♯GA♭AB♭B

Natural minor scales

The natural minor scale has a darker, more serious sound. Every major key has a matching minor key that shares the same notes.

CD♭DE♭EFF♯GA♭AB♭B

Minor pentatonic scales

The minor pentatonic scale has only five notes, which makes it easy to play. It is the sound of blues, rock and a lot of improvised solos.

CD♭DE♭EFF♯GA♭AB♭B

Blues scales

The blues scale is the minor pentatonic scale with one extra note, the flat fifth, which gives it the classic bluesy bend.

CD♭DE♭EFF♯GA♭AB♭B

Chromatic scales

The chromatic scale uses every note, one semitone at a time, so it is great for finger practice. This one goes up one octave with sharps and comes back down with flats.

CD♭DE♭EFF♯GA♭AB♭B

Chromatic scale, 2 octavess

The two octave chromatic scale uses every note for two octaves. It goes up with sharps and comes back down with flats, and it is a good workout for your fingers and your breath.
