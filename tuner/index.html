Saxophone tuner

Play a long note and see the note and how sharp or flat it is. It shows the written note for your sax.

How to use it

  1. Choose alto, soprano or tenor.
  2. Press Start tuner and allow the microphone.
  3. Play a long, steady note. The needle shows if you are flat (left) or sharp (right). It turns green when you are in tune.

Your sound is analysed on your device only and never recorded or sent anywhere. See the common problems guide if you are always sharp or flat.
