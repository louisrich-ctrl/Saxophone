A simple saxophone practice routine

Short daily practice beats one long session. Pick the length that fits your day.

10 minutes

  1. Long tones (2 min): hold a few easy notes, steady and full. See the long tone exercise.
  2. One scale (3 min): play it slowly with the metronome, up and down.
  3. One song (5 min): pick a tune from Simple songs and play along with the microphone.

20 minutes

  1. Long tones (3 min): low, middle and high notes.
  2. Scales (6 min): two scales, slowly and evenly. Try any key.
  3. Arpeggios (4 min): the Major or Minor chord in the same key as your scale.
  4. Songs (7 min): one tune you know and one you are learning.

30 minutes

  1. Long tones and tuning (5 min): check your pitch with the tuner.
  2. Scales (8 min): two or three scales, then the chromatic scale for finger practice.
  3. Arpeggios (5 min): maj7, m7 and 7 chords if you are working on jazz.
  4. Songs (10 min): new tune first, then an old favourite.
  5. Finish (2 min): something fun, then wipe out your sax.

Tips

  • Start slowly. Speed comes from accuracy, not the other way around.
  • Practise the hard part, not just the parts you like.
  • Play every day, even for ten minutes.
  • Record yourself now and then and listen back.

Your teacher can change this plan to suit what you are working on.
